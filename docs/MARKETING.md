# POTIFY Website marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Returning members and developers integrating a project. **Offer.** Give visitors one reliable way to move from a POTIFY project to the next.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Record one iPhone journey from the main hub into a free utility and back. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Open one project from the directory. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 10 qualified project opens. |

### Sales test

Give the store referral and the utility referral separate campaign labels. Proposed 28-day sales test is one platform-confirmed fulfilled order. Attribute a fulfilled merchandise order to the hub referral after the shop supplies an order export.

### Measurement and decision

Use `website-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** A clean public project directory is useful while the production website and its private PR still need authorized source access. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
